How it works

Run Wallet Screening in three steps

The workflow is built for quick pre-transaction checks: paste an address, let the app compare on-chain behavior with AML guidance, then review the result before you act.

1

Paste the wallet

Enter an address or transaction hash for the account you want to review. The app does not ask for a seed phrase, signature, or wallet connection.

2

Check chain activity

Wallet Screening reads public transaction history, counterparties, contract interactions, and explorer-visible movement on networks such as Ethereum.

3

Review the context

Open the result in the app to see risk indicators, supported-chain coverage, timing, and any cost or usage limits before taking the next step.

What it checks

Why screen a wallet before you interact?

Sanctions & watchlists

Check whether a wallet has exposure to OFAC sanctions, restricted entities, or wallet clusters that deserve extra review.

On-chain exposure

Trace public activity through a block explorer style view, including counterparties, token flows, contract calls, and high-risk source patterns.

Read-only safety

You screen by wallet address, so private keys stay out of the flow and no approval or transaction can be triggered by the checker.

FAQ

Wallet Screening questions

01

What is Wallet Screening?

Wallet Screening is a read-only DeFi checker for reviewing a crypto address or transaction hash before you interact with it.

02

What does it look for?

It looks for sanctions exposure, suspicious counterparties, mixer-related movement, risky contract activity, and other on-chain signals used in blockchain analytics.

03

Do I need to connect a wallet?

No. You paste an address or transaction hash. Wallet Screening is non-custodial and read-only, so there is nothing to approve or sign.

04

Which chains and assets are supported?

The app is built for major EVM wallet and token activity, with supported networks listed on the supported chains page.

05

How long does a check take?

Timing depends on the network, address history, and live data availability. The app shows the result state as the screening runs.

06

What does it cost?

Open the app to see any current pricing, limits, or access requirements before you run a screening.
